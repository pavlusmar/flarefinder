# How to turn account evidence into a commercial hypothesis

These patterns were extracted by comparing the ten account plans. Use each pattern as a question. A reference account supplies an analogy. Only the target dossier can establish a fact about the current prospect.

The order matters. Establish the revenue unit and competitive advantage first. Identify the initiative and who owns its workload. Compare the incumbent with a technically compatible alternative and measure the effect on an actual user or operator. Separate a reason to ask from evidence of a purchase.

## 1. Map the monetised event before modelling traffic.

Fresha can earn a fee on an eligible first-client booking. Form3 moves from integration fees to live processing revenue.

[Fresha](references/fresha.md) · [Form3](references/form3.md)

**Use when** The company discloses a billable event, price mechanism or transition to recurring billing.

**Exception** One booking or cleared payment may create multiple requests and may use an intermediary.

**Evidence to seek** Pricing terms, exclusions, integration owner and event denominators.

**Common failure** Multiplying payment value or visits by an assumed infrastructure share.

**Ask** Which completed event changes revenue, and how many calls on the path can your team actually influence?

## 2. Find the technical boundary of the competitive advantage.

Ably sells reliable realtime transport. Harmonic sells employee AI security. Oxa sells autonomy where embedded operation and assurance matter.

[Ably](references/ably.md) · [Harmonic Security](references/harmonic-security.md) · [Oxa](references/oxa.md)

**Use when** The proposed replacement touches the reason customers buy the product.

**Exception** A separately operated integration, control plane or partner service may still be addressable.

**Evidence to seek** Customer value proposition, workload allocation and the incumbent design.

**Common failure** Pitching the core product back to its maker.

**Ask** Which supporting service would you buy if it released engineers for the part customers pay you to build?

## 3. Name the operator, budget owner and beneficiary separately.

An ElevenLabs developer integration may generate downstream consumption. Oxa deployments can involve several operators and shared responsibilities.

[ElevenLabs](references/elevenlabs.md) · [Ably](references/ably.md) · [Oxa](references/oxa.md)

**Use when** Customers, partners or private deployments operate a significant part of the production path.

**Exception** Partner-enabled demand can be commercially valuable without being direct account spend.

**Evidence to seek** Deployment guide, responsibility boundaries, contract or operating owner.

**Common failure** Attributing all downstream accounts, traffic or devices to the platform vendor.

**Ask** Whose account pays for this component when an integration goes live?

## 4. Treat a product launch as evidence of a workflow change.

Research Grid changes the credit analyst's comparison workflow. An Attio interface exposes a permissioned workspace. A MUBI release creates territory-specific eligibility.

[9fin](references/9fin.md) · [Attio](references/attio.md) · [MUBI](references/mubi.md)

**Use when** There is a live feature, release note or operator guide with a discernible user action.

**Exception** A launch can run on an established platform with adequate controls and no new infrastructure decision.

**Evidence to seek** Release date, interaction path, dependency and observable failure or delay.

**Common failure** Equating an MCP server with inference routing or a release with budget.

**Ask** What does the new user action depend on, and which part limits adoption today?

## 5. Use hiring to investigate a shared capability.

A shared platform remit at 9fin suggests a reusable component for several teams. Tessl's actual charging and retention policies give stronger evidence of workload boundaries.

[9fin](references/9fin.md) · [Tessl](references/tessl.md)

**Use when** A dated, employer-specific role describes outcomes and internal consumers.

**Exception** A role can be filled, cancelled or specify desired background unrelated to production.

**Evidence to seek** Original vacancy or identified mirror, remit, corroborating product and architecture evidence.

**Common failure** Treating a desired skill as a deployed vendor, open tender or signed budget.

**Ask** What do product teams repeat today, and which components have already been chosen?

## 6. Ask what incremental value survives the incumbent.

Attio lists Cloudflare as a subprocessor. Form3's isolation objectives constrain any shared dependency. Harmonic's SIEM handoff is narrower than workforce DLP replacement.

[Attio](references/attio.md) · [Form3](references/form3.md) · [Harmonic Security](references/harmonic-security.md)

**Use when** Existing controls, providers or internal components are documented.

**Exception** A vendor disclosure rarely identifies exact products, paths or purchased capacity.

**Evidence to seek** Subprocessor list, integration documentation, resilience design and baseline outcome.

**Common failure** Recommending a greenfield deployment to an existing customer or calling current controls inadequate.

**Ask** What would a new component improve against the current control, and what would it make harder?

## 7. Separate expansion from permission to serve a workload.

MUBI must honour territorial rights, voice processing may require regional routing and Oxa partner sites have local control boundaries.

[MUBI](references/mubi.md) · [ElevenLabs](references/elevenlabs.md) · [Oxa](references/oxa.md)

**Use when** A proposed cross-region path depends on content rights, residency, contracts or operating authority.

**Exception** Public assets or synthetic test traffic can have different constraints than restricted production data.

**Evidence to seek** Region-specific terms, entitlement checks, contract permissions and process owners.

**Common failure** Treating geographic growth as authorization to centralize sensitive workloads.

**Ask** Which traffic may cross this boundary and which must remain in the current region?

## 8. Distinguish retention, delivery and inference costs.

Tessl's retention change affects accumulated byte-months. Speech compute and audio transport have different bottlenecks. A model gateway manages compatible requests.

[Tessl](references/tessl.md) · [ElevenLabs](references/elevenlabs.md) · [9fin](references/9fin.md)

**Use when** Fixed commitments, stored data, egress or compute scale with different workload units.

**Exception** An existing bundled contract, locality rule or model-specific dependency can eliminate savings.

**Evidence to seek** Retention cohorts, reads, egress, call volume, supported models, current net contract rates.

**Common failure** Inferring model spend or GPU demand from a developer tool, asset or MCP launch.

**Ask** Which cost changes when users double or retention extends while daily runs stay flat?

## 9. Choose a measurable outcome on the purchasing team's path.

False positives can block authorised Fresha demand. Faster simulator qualification might bring a bank live sooner. MUBI needs conversion among eligible viewers.

[Fresha](references/fresha.md) · [Form3](references/form3.md) · [MUBI](references/mubi.md)

**Use when** A technical measurement can be paired with a business event and a relevant control group.

**Exception** Latency changes might have no impact on completion, certification or renewal.

**Evidence to seek** Baseline distribution, eligible cohort, owner, control path and success or abort criteria.

**Common failure** Claiming a faster endpoint automatically raises revenue.

**Ask** What comparison would show the engineering change caused a business improvement?

## 10. Separate growth evidence, initiative readiness and vendor timing.

A financing announcement, a joint venture and a new security integration each justify different questions. None alone confirms a purchasing decision.

[9fin](references/9fin.md) · [Oxa](references/oxa.md) · [Harmonic Security](references/harmonic-security.md)

**Use when** Evidence dates identify current activity and a plausible stakeholder.

**Exception** An implementation can already be contracted or an old hiring signal can be stale.

**Evidence to seek** Dated milestones, named owner, architectural decision window and evidence of evaluation or budget.

**Common failure** Treating investment size as a sales opportunity amount or close date.

**Ask** What decision remains open, when will it close and who can still influence it?

## 11. Land on a reversible workload and expand only after proof.

A simulator, a single permissioned interface or a public artwork path can be tested without putting payment clearing, the CRM record or film entitlement at risk.

[Form3](references/form3.md) · [Attio](references/attio.md) · [MUBI](references/mubi.md)

**Use when** A separable pilot has representative traffic, baseline, owner and rollback path.

**Exception** A nonrepresentative sandbox can hide contractual, availability and volume constraints.

**Evidence to seek** Pilot acceptance criteria, stop condition and evidence the adjacent team has the same constraint.

**Common failure** Presenting a roadmap of product cross-sells before validating the first workload.

**Ask** Which single path would give a decision in one test, and which adjacent path would that evidence cover?

## 12. Build explicit stopping conditions into the account decision.

Core overlap, customer ownership or absent authority can outweigh a strong technical similarity. A well-qualified decision to defer can be the best result.

[Ably](references/ably.md) · [Harmonic Security](references/harmonic-security.md) · [Oxa](references/oxa.md)

**Use when** No owned commercial gap survives the incumbent, alternatives and ownership checks.

**Exception** New ownership, a documented service failure or a distinct approved initiative can reopen the question.

**Evidence to seek** Negative evidence, unknowns and a specific observable reopening condition.

**Common failure** Adding another pillar solely to make the account appear attractive.

**Ask** What new evidence would change the decision to defer, and where would it appear?
