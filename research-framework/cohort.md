Today's unicorns: privately held digital product companies with sourced valuation >= USD 1bn. Tomorrow's unicorns: eligible promising startups and high-growth scale-ups; do not imply future valuation is guaranteed. Unknown valuation is unknown, not proof of sub-unicorn status. Public mature digital natives are outside this target strategy. Separate market eligibility, business maturity, commercial potential and engagement readiness. Do not force product hypotheses or scores. Keep discovery holdouts out of examples and manually seeded territory. See reference-cases.md for existing and additional preliminary cases.
